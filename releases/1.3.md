# Freestyle Studio 1.3

- Preview your Shorts with YouTube, Instagram Reels, or TikTok controls while filming. Choose the platform before recording; show or hide the overlay while filming.
- The preview has breathing room above and below, with app navigation below the recorded frame.
- Choose **Edit profile…** to save a handle, display name, and profile photo or logo for each platform. Profiles stay on your Mac.
- App overlays and profile details are preview-only and do not appear in saved video.

Platform layouts are representative and may vary by phone and app version. This release adds local preview profiles; it does not connect social accounts.
