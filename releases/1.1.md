Keep your script still until you are ready to read. The new **Auto-scroll on record** button in Studio Script Settings remembers your choice and preserves your reading position when turned off.

Switch between **Camera**, **Screen**, and **Both** during a recording. The controls stay beneath Stop, and Control–Option–1, 2, and 3 switch views from other apps. Returning to Both keeps your camera framing.

Choose your camera and screen before recording. Curtain Call disables views whose sources are unavailable during a take.
